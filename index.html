Remove every wall?

Click or drag along a line between cells to add a wall; do the same on a wall to remove it. States are coloured automatically. The capitol (dot) is found from each state's symmetry. With Show route on hover on, rest the mouse on a cell for a second to see its cheapest route to a capitol. Clues turn green when the cheapest trip to a capitol equals the clue, red otherwise.
