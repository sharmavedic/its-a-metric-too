Remove every wall?

Click or drag along a line between cells to add a wall; do the same on a wall to remove it. States are coloured automatically. The capitol (dot) is found from each state's symmetry. With Show route on hover on, rest the mouse on a cell for a second to see its cheapest route to a capitol. Clues turn green when the cheapest trip to a capitol equals the clue, red otherwise. Click inside a cell to mark it Solve or Constraint and set its allowed state sizes and travel costs. Found arrangements are drawn in orange; use the arrows or the ← → keys to page through them. Walls and cell settings save in this browser.
